How it all started

My crochet journey started unexpectedly in 2022, during a challenging time when my husband was hospitalised for an extended period.

While waiting for visiting hours, I needed something to keep my hands busy and my mind occupied. That's when I picked up a crochet hook and discovered a love for creating things one stitch at a time.

What started as a way to pass the time soon turned into something much more meaningful.

My very first order came from a friend who asked me to make a unicorn. I took on the challenge, not quite realising where that one little order would lead. Before I knew it, one request turned into another, and my little hobby began to grow into a passion.

Today, crochet is more than just a hobby. It's a creative outlet, a source of joy and a way to turn a simple ball of yarn into something special. Every creation is handmade with care, patience and a little bit of love.
